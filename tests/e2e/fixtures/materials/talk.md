# Rooftop beekeeping in Kyiv

A two-year study of honey bee colonies kept on city rooftops.

## Method

We followed 48 hives on 6 rooftops in Kyiv from April 2024 to September 2025,
weighing each hive weekly and counting varroa mites every month.

## Results

Rooftop colonies produced 31 kg of honey per hive per season, against 24 kg
for the suburban control group. Winter losses were 9 percent.

## Prepared answers

Q: How many hives did you study?
A: We studied 48 hives on 6 rooftops in Kyiv over two seasons.

Q: Why did you put the hives on rooftops?
A: Rooftops have fewer pesticides and a long flowering season from street trees, mainly linden.

Q: What were the winter losses?
A: Winter losses were 9 percent, lower than the national average.
